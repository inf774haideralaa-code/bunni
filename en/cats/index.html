Before you enter

Please read the rules before entering the cat room, for their comfort and your safety

  • 1Wash or sanitize your hands before touching the cats
  • 2Don't pick a cat up by force — let it come to you
  • 3Only feed the cats with the café's special treats
  • 4Keep your voice low and avoid sudden moves
  • 5Please don't wake sleeping cats
  • 6Children must stay under their guardian's supervision
